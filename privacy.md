# SubTrackr — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

SubTrackr is a personal finance app for tracking subscriptions, income and cash flow. It is currently operated by its developer for personal use.

## What data the app handles

- **Data you enter** — subscriptions, income, categories, notes and settings. This is stored in a local database on your device, and (if you sign in) backed up to the app's database hosted on Supabase, protected so that each account can only access its own rows.
- **Account data** — if you create an account, your email address and a securely hashed password are stored by Supabase for sign-in.
- **Bank data (optional)** — if you choose to connect a bank account, SubTrackr receives **read-only** account information (account details and transactions) through **Enable Banking**, a licensed open-banking provider. Your bank login credentials are entered only on your bank's own pages and are never seen by, transmitted to, or stored by SubTrackr. Bank consents are time-limited (around 90 days) and can be revoked at any time from the app or via your bank.

## What the app does not do

- No data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are included.
- Payments are never initiated — bank access is read-only.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** (database hosting and authentication) — SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified.
- **Enable Banking** (open-banking account information) — regulated provider; see their privacy policy at enablebanking.com.

## Your choices

- Bank connections are optional; the app is fully usable with manual entry only.
- Disconnecting a bank stops all further imports.
- To delete your cloud data, delete your account or contact the address below; data on your own device can be removed by uninstalling the app.
